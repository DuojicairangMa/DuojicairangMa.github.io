About

Hi! I am Duojicairang Ma, a Ph.D. student at Lanzhou University. My research interests lie at the intersection of neural dynamics, intelligent optimization, and multi-robot systems.

My research aims to develop efficient and theoretically interpretable computational methods by integrating dynamical systems theory, optimization, and artificial intelligence. In particular, I am interested in neural-dynamics-based optimization methods for real-time intelligent systems and cooperative robotic systems.

More broadly, I am exploring how learning mechanisms can be integrated with dynamical optimization methods, including learning-enhanced numerical solvers, deep-learning optimization, and intelligent algorithms for complex robotic and manufacturing systems.

Research

Neural Dynamics and Intelligent Optimization

Developing continuous- and discrete-time neural dynamics for real-time optimization, time-varying problems, and intelligent computation, with emphasis on convergence, robustness, and numerical efficiency.

Multi-Robot Cooperative Intelligence

Studying distributed optimization, coordination, and control for multi-robot systems, including constrained motion generation, redundancy resolution, robust control, and cooperative task execution.

Learning-Enhanced Optimization

Investigating the connection between dynamical systems and modern machine learning, including learnable optimization, deep-learning optimization, generalization enhancement, and intelligent numerical solvers.
