Bio

Hi! I am Duojicairang Ma, a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Lanzhou University. My research focuses on developing theoretically interpretable intelligent algorithms by integrating dynamical systems theory, optimization, and artificial intelligence.

My current research interests include neural dynamics-based optimization, distributed cooperative control for multi-robot systems, and learning-enhanced optimization methods for deep neural networks. I aim to bridge mathematical theories of dynamical systems with emerging intelligent computing paradigms.

More broadly, I am exploring efficient and robust optimization frameworks for intelligent systems, including neural-dynamics-driven numerical solvers, deep learning optimization, generalization enhancement, and AI-enabled robotic manufacturing applications.
