Bio

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. degree at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Lanzhou University. My research centers on exploring how dynamical principles and optimization theories can inspire the development of efficient and reliable intelligent algorithms.

My work investigates neural-dynamics-based computational methods, including continuous- and discrete-time neurodynamic models for complex optimization problems, as well as their extensions to distributed intelligence and cooperative control in multi-robot systems. In parallel, I explore optimization mechanisms for deep learning models, with the goal of improving training efficiency, generalization capability, and robustness of intelligent systems.

Through the integration of dynamical systems, optimization theory, and artificial intelligence, I aim to establish principled computational frameworks that connect mathematical foundations with emerging intelligent technologies.
